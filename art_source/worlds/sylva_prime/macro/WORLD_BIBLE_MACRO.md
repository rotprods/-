## 6. Narrative material pressure

**DOCUMENTED secret:** some colonists voluntarily asked to integrate; others were connected without understanding the contract.

**Macro implication — INFERRED:** Puerto del Injerto cannot read as a conventional prefab colony merely placed on a forest floor. Human structure must visibly clamp, graft, bridge and negotiate with living load paths. The distinction between consent, maintenance and extraction must be legible through construction interfaces, not exposition alone.

## 7. Art-direction contract for macro foundation

### CANON
- Neural forest.
- Structural roots.
- Membranes.
- Graft scaffolds.
- Layered foliage.
- Wood percussion, multiple breaths and phrases arriving through separated roots.
- Navigation risk: organic forms must preserve slopes, widths and route language.

### PROPOSAL — shape language
- **Primary living load:** tapered bifurcating roots, compression arches and buttresses.
- **Secondary living interface:** membrane spans between growth nodes, tension rather than arbitrary hard-surface panels.
- **Human intervention:** orthogonal/triangulated graft scaffolds attached by explicit clamps; narrow dimensional regularity against irregular growth.
- **Route language:** repeated physical clearances and geometric signal counts; color is redundant information, never the only information.

### PROPOSAL — manufacturing/growth causality
- Living roots branch according to hierarchical radii; child structures cannot visually exceed parent load without an explicit support reason.
- Graft hardware transfers load through clamps and broad contact areas instead of appearing glued to bark.
- Membranes connect anchor points and imply tension/sag.
- Human repair history should eventually show replaceable brackets, straps, drainage and inspection access.

### Forbidden drift
- random greeble;
- generic purple alien fantasy;
- uniformly glowing plants;
- roots crossing gameplay lanes as visual spaghetti without growth/load reason;
- sci-fi panels floating on biological surfaces;
- noise-driven roughness/wear without causal process;
- using decals or color to compensate for ambiguous macro silhouette.

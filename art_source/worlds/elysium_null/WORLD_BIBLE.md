## 1. NORTH STAR LOCAL

Create a world-scale artificial habitat whose first read is immaculate hospitality and whose second read is coercive control. ELYSIUM NULL must be identifiable without logos through measured repetition, pristine construction logic, controlled vegetation, absence as composition, and very sparse human irregularities that become disproportionately meaningful.

The world must remain game-readable: minimalism is not empty level design. Routes, cover, combat tells, services, state changes, anomalies and human traces must carry narrative density without falling back to random greeble or generic sci-fi noise.

### Acceptance intent

- A player at close / medium / far camera distances reads the world as ELYSIUM NULL rather than “generic white sci-fi”.
- Every repeated module has a service/manufacturing reason.
- Irregularity is localized and causal, never sprayed procedurally as fake realism.
- The 48 m EDEN arena preserves guaranteed corridors while supporting moving modules.
- World geometry remains divisible into streaming cells and modular kits.
- Hero assets keep editable Blender source, deterministic generator, portable exchange export and measurable QA evidence.

# 3. CANONICAL REGIONS

## 3.1 Avenida del Recibimiento

**CANON:** flawless services with no visible inhabitants.

Production interpretation:
- Arrival axis and first hospitality test.
- Repetition must read as intentional automation, not copy/paste laziness.
- Transit, signage, accommodation and public-service interfaces should already be operational despite the population void.
- Sparse evidence of previous human presence is a high-value storytelling channel.

## 3.2 Casas sin Desgaste

**CANON:** homes personalized from ancient profiles.

Production interpretation:
- Domestic modules should be customized by obsolete preference records rather than current occupants.
- “Personalization” can therefore become uncanny through frozen tastes, preserved arrangements and maintenance that prevents normal aging.
- Variation must derive from profile archetypes and service systems, not decorative randomness.

## 3.3 Jardín de EDEN

**CANON:** control center of an imposed perfection.

**CANON arena:** geometric garden, **48 m diameter**, with modules that change position between attacks while minimum corridors remain guaranteed.

Production interpretation:
- This is the geometry proof for ELYSIUM’s thesis: movable perfection as a weapon.
- Arena changes must be telegraphed spatially and preserve combat legibility.
- The garden needs strong topological states rather than decorative animation only.

# 11. FORMAL ART DIRECTION — PROPOSAL v0.1

These values are reversible until creative review.

## Art pillars

1. **Hospitality without inhabitants** — every public system behaves as if the city is occupied.
2. **Perfect repeatability** — repetition is a world mechanic and cultural artifact, not asset reuse hidden from the player.
3. **Control through comfort** — barriers, circulation and correction initially use the visual language of care.
4. **Human imperfection as signal** — a repaired bench, displaced object or weathered grip can matter more than a wall of decoration.
5. **Maintenance is architecture** — service access, drainage, nutrient feed and transit are integrated into clean macro-forms.

## Shape language

- Primary: long orthogonal planes, broad arcs, circles and controlled cylindrical service cores.
- Secondary: recessed slots, shadow reveals, continuous rails, aligned canopies.
- Forbidden drift: exposed random pipes, military greeble, excessive panelization, fantasy ornament, gratuitous cyberpunk signage.

## Proportion language

- Human clearances remain physically plausible.
- Public volumes intentionally overscale humans by approximately 8–25× in height at macro landmarks, while interaction surfaces return to human scale.
- Repeated modules share grids and heights; exceptional proportions indicate state, fault or forbidden human intervention.

## Silhouette hierarchy

1. EDEN / world-control landmarks.
2. Arcology massing and transit axes.
3. Repeated garden / service modules.
4. NPC/enemy readable silhouettes.
5. Sparse anomaly objects.

## Material families — proposal

- White ceramic/mineral composite: dominant envelope; matte-to-satin, never plastic toy-white.
- Satin metal: structural/service spine, low visual noise.
- Dark recess material: depth/readability, used sparingly.
- Designed vegetation: dark restrained greens rather than saturated decorative foliage.
- Warm repair material / service amber: rare, high-information anomaly and maintenance channel.

No project-wide numeric PBR ranges are declared canon before material calibration under target lighting/engine.

## Wear logic

Normal ELYSIUM surfaces are actively maintained. Wear should therefore be:
- extremely localized;
- linked to access failure, hidden occupancy, human repair, impossible maintenance zone, or deliberate anomaly;
- directional and causal.

Do **not** apply uniform scratches, dirt overlays or generic edge wear.

# 14. REALISM / ANTI-CGI CONTRACT

Reject:
- random greeble;
- uniform procedural grime;
- arbitrary decals;
- scale-less panels;
- impossible cantilevers without structural logic;
- vegetation placed only for composition without a maintenance/nutrient system;
- fake “luxury” made only from gloss;
- perfect symmetry where human intervention is the narrative signal.

Require:
- buildable assemblies;
- service clearances;
- drainage/nutrient paths where relevant;
- plausible hand/foot contact zones;
- consistent human scale;
- construction joints where macro parts actually meet;
- only causal irregularity.

## Local art direction contract

**Shape** — concentric incomplete rings, radial bridges and stable nodes. Curves are structural routes, not decorative halos.

**Proportion** — human/avatar is deliberately small against 34/52/70 m ring diameters; readable bridge widths and repeated joint scales preserve navigation.

**Silhouette** — three nested rings must remain identifiable without materials. The outer ring carries the dominant horizon; inner rings tighten toward the negotiation/boss core.

**Material causality** — black mineral behaves as compressed/load-bearing mass; living bronze appears at joints, valves and adaptive interfaces where movement or signal transfer has a function. Prior-world echoes are inserts, not a texture collage.

**Construction / evolution** — treat the structure as grown/assembled by a transit organism: continuous load arcs, replaceable joint organs, seams at route transitions and visible maintenance logic.

**Forbidden drift** — generic neon rings, Tron shorthand, random greebles, uniform glow, impossible unsupported bridges, decorative sci-fi panels, unbounded copies of previous-world motifs.

## 7. Art Direction Contract

### Pillar A — precision against uncertainty

Human infrastructure tries to measure an environment that refuses a single future. Geometry is measured, calibrated and serviceable; temporal phenomena are bounded, not noisy chaos.

### Pillar B — mechanical time, living time

Mechanical rings/gears/escapements contrast with grass, insect emergence, animal tracks and growth. Avoid giant decorative clocks with no function.

### Pillar C — the future leaves physical receipts

A temporal event is communicated through duplicated state, delayed footprint, shifted shadow, mechanical register or observable wear—not arbitrary holographic noise.

### Shape language

- primary: circles, rings, radial tracks, nested arcs;
- secondary: forked paths, bifurcated frames, paired offset silhouettes;
- tertiary: calibrated teeth, mechanical index marks, service latches;
- negative-space priority: sky and horizon remain visible across prairie.

### Proportion language

- settlement horizontal and low;
- observatory instruments oversized but mechanically plausible;
- AEON geometry grows vertically/branch-like only where it represents possibility branching;
- human doors, steps, rails and maintenance access remain real scale.

### Material families

CANON/global-compatible:

- ivory technical ceramic;
- dark structural/technical metal;
- technical textiles;
- maintenance marks.

Aurora PROPOSAL additions:

- weathered precision steel;
- low-expansion timing alloy / aged brass-like service components;
- optical glass and coated lenses;
- matte instrument polymer only where function demands it;
- cool prairie soil/stone;
- fibrous two-shadow grass;
- translucent echo-state material restricted to temporal evidence, never generic decoration.

### Wear causality

- wind abrasion on exposed rings;
- handling polish on calibration wheels;
- lubricant/grease at mechanical pivots;
- dust accumulation in leeward cavities;
- moisture/condensation on lenses and sheltered steel;
- grass contact on lower structures;
- no uniform edge wear or random scratches.

### Lighting

- aurora is environmental context, not universal emissive fill;
- gameplay signaling uses duplicated form + sound + motion in addition to color;
- avoid bloom masking anticipation.

### Forbidden drift

- generic neon city language;
- uncontrolled glitch shaders;
- whole-scene double exposure;
- random clocks as decoration;
- impossible floating greebles without structural support;
- global time rewind;
- temporal effects whose state cannot be read before damage.

# EXOVANT 2950 — WORLD DENSITY ×100 ALGORITHM v1

Status: ACTIVE
Command: `/EXOVANT-X100`
Scope: all EXOVANT 2950 world-production branches; current execution target PELAGOS.

## North Star
Maximize credible world depth per production hour without sacrificing quality, coherence, performance, originality, or reproducibility. Worlds must feel as if they existed before the player arrived and would continue to exist without them.

## Objective
`WORLD_COMPLETENESS = MACRO_WORLD × MESO_ARCHITECTURE × MICRO_ASSETS × ECOLOGY × CIVILIZATION × GAMEPLAY × MATERIAL_CAUSALITY × VARIATION × TEMPORAL_STATES × STORYTELLING × AUDIO_VISUAL_LANGUAGE × OPTIMIZATION × REUSABILITY × QA`

A near-zero critical dimension blocks world completion regardless of excellence elsewhere.

## Scale Pyramid
- L0 Planetary: horizon, geography, climate, biomes, atmosphere, megastructures.
- L1 Regional: regions, settlements, routes, landmarks, geological/ecological networks.
- L2 District: districts, ports, bases, factories, temples, corridors.
- L3 Architectural: buildings, rooms, bridges, facades, interiors, structure, access systems.
- L4 Prop/Ecology: tools, machines, domestic objects, flora/fauna, waste, resources, cultural artifacts.
- L5 Microdetail: joints, welds, cracks, deposits, fibers, wear, residue, manufacturing variation.

WORLD_DENSITY_GATE fails if the world exists only at L0–L2.

## Mandatory Master Asset Graph
Every important node should resolve toward:
`BASE_ASSET → VARIANT_SET → STATE_SET → LOD_SET → COLLISION → MATERIAL_SET → PLACEMENT_RULES → GAMEPLAY_TAGS → STORY_TAGS`.

Top domains:
- BIOME: terrain, flora, fauna, weather.
- CIVILIZATION: architecture, infrastructure, industry, transport, domestic, cultural.
- GAMEPLAY: traversal, combat, puzzle, cover, interaction.
- STORY: original state, modification, current degradation/adaptation, repair, abandonment.

## Expansion Contract
For every important family:
- hero/support/systemic role;
- semantic small/medium/large where meaningful;
- pristine/used/damaged/abandoned where causally justified;
- regional/manufacturing variants where justified;
- traversable/blocked/destroyed/interactive states when gameplay-relevant;
- LOD0/1/2 and optional HLOD;
- collision/query policy;
- material response variants;
- snapping/modular interfaces;
- deterministic placement rules;
- silhouette, material-causality, gameplay-readability and optimization gates.

## Variation Rule
No random-grunge variation. Variation must derive from environment, manufacturing, age, usage, damage, repair, culture or biology.

## Culture DNA
Every civilization family should encode:
manufacturing language, construction logic, ergonomics, ornamentation, repair language, resource constraints, color logic, iconography, social hierarchy and technology level.

## Ecology Rule
Represent resource → primary consumer → predator → scavenger → decomposer, including physical effects on terrain, vegetation, architecture, routes, carcasses, sound, light and gameplay.

## History as Geometry
Important zones must show at least three simultaneous eras:
ERA_0 original construction → ERA_1 modification/occupation → ERA_2 current degradation/adaptation.

## Distribution
Target 5% HERO / 20% SUPPORT / 75% SYSTEMIC-PROCEDURAL. Manual one-offs require identity value high enough to justify cost.

## Combinatorial Kit Gate
- <10 configurations: poor
- 10–30: acceptable
- 30–100: strong
- 100+: production-grade systemic kit

Core structural kits should target 100+ credible configurations.

## Production Loop
RESYNC → OWNERSHIP → WORLD BIBLE → COVERAGE MATRIX → highest-value gap → CLAIM → North Star/DoD/asset graph/variation graph/QA → base family → geometry/player-scale/gameplay QA → variants/states → material causality → narrative wear → procedural rules → LOD/optimization → engine qualification → receipt → coverage update → handoff → next gap.

## Permanent Iteration
Never stop at `asset complete`. Continue family → ecosystem → region → missing domain until BLOCKED, OWNERSHIP_CONFLICT, QUALITY_GATE_FAILURE, significant unauthorized cost/destruction risk, or WORLD_COMPLETE.

## Planning Ranges
Large main world: 20–40 architecture families; 10–25 infrastructure; 30–80 props; 10–30 vegetation; 5–20 fauna; 10–30 machinery; 5–20 cultural; 5–15 traversal; 5–15 damage/destruction; 5–15 hero landmarks. Typical family: 3–12 variants, 2–5 material states, 2–4 LODs. These are revisable planning ranges, not canon.

## Procedural Multiplier Priority
modular kits → Geometry Nodes → deterministic generators → material variation → decals → scatter → biome rules → HLOD → prefab assemblies → manual one-offs.

## AAAA Hardness Contract
Reject AI slop, plastic CGI, random grunge, perfect symmetry, meaningless greeble, floating geometry, uniform wear, unmotivated emissives, unrealistic intersections, uniform material response, unscaled detail and decorative complexity without structural logic.

Require mass, weight, construction/load logic, material thickness, assembly, causal wear, environmental response, scale, access/maintenance, damage and age.

## Per-Family DoD
Semantic purpose; stable IDs; validated dimensions/player scale/silhouette/manufacturing logic; UV0; materials; pivot; clean transforms; collision policy; gameplay clearance; LODs; variants; states; optimization; engine import/qualification; visual/technical QA; receipt; persisted source/export; claim/handoff.

Engine-gated items may remain explicitly pending when the production engine is unresolved; they must never be falsely marked PASS.

## Completeness Labels
<15% BLOCKOUT; 15–35% PLAYABLE FOUNDATION; 35–65% PRODUCTION WORLD; 65–85% DENSE AAA WORLD; 85–95% AAAA WORLD CANDIDATE; 95%+ RELEASE CANDIDATE.

## Current Blockers Policy
Known global blockers such as unresolved production-engine qualification do not stop Blender-side source, family, LOD, collision-policy, procedural, optimization-prep or documentation work. They only block engine-qualified claims.
